Service areas

Websites for small businesses in NJ, NY, PA, and CT.

The Site Shack is a New Jersey studio that builds phone-first sites for local trades and shops across four states. Work happens on a call and in the draft — you do not need to drive to an office to get a clear front door online.

NJ

New Jersey

Home base. We build for New Jersey landscapers, plumbers, car washes, HVAC shops, and other small businesses that need hours, a phone number, and a page a customer can trust on a phone.

Towns from North Jersey through the Shore and South Jersey are a natural fit: the copy names the place you actually serve, not a vague “statewide” claim that confuses searchers.

NY

New York

We work with New York small businesses that need a simple site — especially shops and trades serving a town, borough, or county rather than a national brand site.

The same rule applies: plain language, local details, and a contact path that reaches a person. Larger rebuilds are quoted before they start.

PA

Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania businesses get the same package: a phone-first site with clear services, hours, and a way to call or request a quote.

Whether you cover one borough or a cluster of towns, the page should say that out loud so neighbors and search engines can tell who you help.

CT

Connecticut

Connecticut shops and trades get a site shaped for how people hire locally — short pages, real photos, and SEO basics that name the work and the towns when they belong.

A simple website starts at $500 and stays online for 5 years. Anything larger is written up before a build begins.

How remote work works

Same process in every state.

You send photos and the facts a stranger would need. We draft a site you can click through. You correct what sounds wrong. Then we launch, test the contact path, and walk you through ordinary edits.

That is why the studio can serve New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, and Connecticut without a storefront in every town: the product is the website, reviewed together on a call.
