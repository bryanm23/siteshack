The approach

People look you up before they walk in.

They want the hours, a phone number, a sense of the place, and proof you do the work. The site is that answer, sitting there at night when someone is deciding who to call in the morning.

The Site Shack works with small businesses in New Jersey. The pages stay few, in an order a customer can follow on a phone. The words sound like the business, because they come from a conversation with you.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Services or a menu, written the way a customer would look for them.

SEO

Built so people can find the business.

Search will not replace a good offer, but a site should still be clear to Google and to a customer on a phone. Every Site Shack build includes the SEO basics that help a New Jersey shop show up for the work it actually does.

Clear titles and pages

Each page gets a plain title and description: the business, the trade, and the town when it belongs there. Headings follow the same order a person would scan.

Local details that matter

Hours, phone, service area, and a place to put a map or address. The page says what you do in the towns you cover, not a pile of keyword stuffing.

Fast on a phone

Search rewards pages that load cleanly on mobile. Layouts are phone-first, images are sized for the web, and tap targets stay easy to hit with a thumb.

Structure engines can read

Sensible URLs, alt text on photos, and structured data for a local business when it fits. You get a site that is ready to be indexed, not a mystery file.

SEO basics ship with the simple site. Ranking for competitive searches, Google Business setup, and paid ads are separate work and quoted before they start.

Process

From a conversation to a site you can run.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A call or a written note. What you sell, who it’s for, and what should happen when someone lands on the site. You send photos and the details a stranger would need.

  2. 02

    Draft

    A site you can click through, with real words. You mark what sounds wrong. One main round of revisions is included, then the loose ends get tied.

  3. 03

    Launch

    Domain connected, phones checked, contact path tested. You leave with a walkthrough for changing hours, prices, or a photo.

  4. 04

    Look after

    Ordinary updates when the season, menu, or services change. Larger additions are quoted on their own before they start.
