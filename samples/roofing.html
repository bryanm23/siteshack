On the page

Repair, replace, or the gutter at the foundation.

A roofing page should help someone describe a leak without climbing up there. It asks where the water shows inside, then separates a patch from a replacement.

Jobs listed

  • Leak tracing: flashing, a missing shingle, a valley.
  • Full replacement when patches are no longer honest.
  • Gutters: clogs, sagging runs, downspouts at the foundation.
  • Photos of what they found, so you can see the repair.
  • A written scope before a tear-off.

How a job starts

  1. Tell them where the water shows up inside.
  2. Send a photo of the ceiling stain and, if you can, the roof from the ground.
  3. They look and say repair or replace.
  4. You approve the work before materials are ordered.

What to send

  • The town, and the age of the roof if you know it.
  • Whether the leak is active.
  • Photos from the ground.
  • If a gutter is overflowing onto the foundation.
