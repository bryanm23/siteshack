On the page

What someone reads while the water is running.

A plumber page gets opened in a hurry. It should say which jobs they take, how a call turns into a window, and what to have ready before the truck leaves.

Jobs on this page

  • Leaks at faucets, toilets, and supply lines.
  • Clogs in a kitchen, a bath, or a main line.
  • Water heater repair and replacement, with fuel and size written down.
  • Fixture swaps: faucet, toilet, disposal.
  • A clear answer on whether the job is today or a scheduled window.

How a call starts

  1. Say what is leaking, or what is not draining.
  2. Say if the water is still on, and which room.
  3. They give a window, not a vague sometime.
  4. You approve the repair before they open a wall.

What to have ready

  • The town.
  • A photo of the leak, or the label on the heater.
  • How old the heater is, if you know.
  • Whether someone will be home for the window.

Before they roll

Questions people ask with a wrench already out.

Do you shut the water off?

If a line is running, yes. Tell them if a shutoff is already closed so they do not spend the first minutes hunting for it.

Will you replace a heater the same day?

Only if the unit and the venting match what is already there. Otherwise they schedule the install and say so on the page.
