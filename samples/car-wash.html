On the page

What a driver can check before they pull in.

A car wash site is used from the driver's seat. It should say what the wash includes, whether interiors need a time, and what the monthly plan actually buys.

What the wash includes

  • Exterior: soap, rinse, and a dry.
  • Interior: vacuum, glass, mats, and a note for pet hair.
  • A monthly plan with a set number of exterior washes.
  • Hours posted so nobody guesses whether the bay is open.
  • Room on the page for one address, left blank in this sample.

How a visit goes

  1. Pull in during the posted hours.
  2. Say exterior, interior, or both.
  3. Wait while they work, or drop the keys if the shop offers that.
  4. Look the car over before you leave.

What to say when you call

  • Exterior only, or interior too.
  • A monthly plan, or a single visit.
  • Pet hair, a car seat, or a spill.
  • Whether you need the car back the same hour.
