The house

A casual room for a long Portuguese dinner.

Atlantic Manor sits at 83 Main Street in Ogdensburg. The kitchen cooks Portuguese and Spanish food, with seafood and steak on the same menu and Brazilian-style rotisserie meats from an open grill. Plates come out large. Sit down inside, call for pickup, or book the private dining room.

  • Indoor seating
  • Pickup
  • Private dining room
  • Happy hour food
  • Cocktails
  • Children’s menu

Open grill

Traditional Portuguese meat and Brazilian-style rodizio, carved from the rotisserie in a casual dining room.

Seafood steakhouse

Paella, lobster, clams, and bacalhau share the menu with a 30-ounce cowboy steak and surf and turf.

The bar

Sangria, a dirty martini, and Super Bock, the Portuguese lager, alongside the happy hour food.
